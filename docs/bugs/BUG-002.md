# BUG-002

## Bug Title

Background sound is white-noise-like, ignores volume, continues after stop, and overpowers speech after meanings.

## First Reported

2026-07-16

## Reproduction Steps

Initial user-reported paths:

1. Select background sound `rain`.
2. Start playback and listen to the background sound.
3. Adjust voice/background volume controls.
4. Click Stop while background audio is playing.
5. Select native language Chinese, target language Japanese, topic Food.
6. Start playback and listen after the app says the meaning `米饭；餐`.
7. Repeat with other language/topic combinations.

## Expected Result

- `rain` should sound recognizably like rain, not generic white noise.
- Voice and background volume controls should affect the corresponding audio.
- Stop should immediately stop voice and background audio.
- Background audio should remain ducked or balanced around speech and should not suddenly become loud enough to cover the voice.

## Actual Result

- `rain` sounds like white noise.
- Audio volume cannot be controlled.
- After clicking Stop, white-noise-like background audio keeps playing.
- With Chinese + Japanese + Food, after `米饭；餐` is spoken, the background noise becomes very loud and covers the voice. Similar behavior is reproducible with other combinations.

## Severity

High. This affects the core audio playback experience and can continue unwanted sound after Stop.

## Investigator Conclusion

Bug Investigator analytically confirmed multiple frontend audio issues in `src/main.tsx`:

- `rain` is generated as raw random noise with occasional spikes, so it is effectively white noise.
- `startSession()` starts background audio, but `stopSession()` does not call `background.stop()`, leaving the audio source/context running after Stop and media-session pause.
- In Recall mode, background ducking is released immediately after the native meaning and before the recall pause, so the background ramps back to full configured volume after phrases like `米饭；餐`.
- Active volume behavior is partly stale/scheduled: background gain updates do not cancel existing ramps, and voice playback uses config captured at session start.

## Root Cause

Frontend audio lifecycle and synthesis issues in `src/main.tsx`:

1. `createNoiseSource()` treats `rain` as unfiltered white noise with random amplitude spikes.
2. `stopSession()` cancels speech but does not stop background audio.
3. Ducking restores background to full configured volume during recall pauses, which can overpower the next voice when the background texture is noise-like.
4. Active gain updates do not cancel scheduled ramps before applying new volume.
