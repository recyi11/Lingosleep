# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want low-friction vocabulary review at night, while resting, or during quiet downtime. They need the interface to stay calm, legible, and predictable on mobile, with enough control to choose languages, voices, timing, and review mode without feeling like they are configuring a studio.

## Product Purpose

LingoSleep helps users review Japanese, Korean, and English vocabulary with paced audio, native-language meanings, examples, background sounds, favorites, history, and lightweight cross-device sync. Success means users can start or resume a review session quickly, trust that progress and vocabulary sources are current, and use the player without visual strain in dim environments.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, nocturnal. The product should feel like a capable learning tool softened for rest time, not a wellness gimmick and not an enterprise dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon cyberpunk, decorative gradients that compete with controls, low-contrast gray text, over-rounded cards, and generic glassy panels. The UI should not imply that sleep alone creates fluency.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary session path obvious from the first screen.
- Use calm contrast: readable in the dark without feeling harsh.
- Treat blue as a state and action color, not decoration.
- Keep controls familiar and dense enough for repeated use.
- Let audio and progress feel reliable before adding flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep motion subtle, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid relying on color alone for selected, disabled, or error states.
